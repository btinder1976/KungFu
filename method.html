The Touchstone Method

A school for students who want more than kick-and-punch classes.

Touchstone Martial Arts should feel different the moment someone lands on the site. The school is presented as serious, traditional, practical, and built around understanding the art rather than collecting flashy movements.

The method emphasizes structured learning, clear fundamentals, close-range application, and apprenticeship-style growth where students deepen their skill by learning how to explain and help others.

How Training Is Framed

Simple ideas, trained deeply.

基

Foundation First

Students begin by learning how stance, body alignment, hands, feet, and centerline create useful Wing Chun mechanics.

用

Practical Use

Training is described around what works under pressure: direct movement, touch, timing, structure, and close-range decisions.

傳

Apprenticeship Culture

Advanced students help newer students, reinforcing the idea that teaching is one of the best ways to truly understand Kung Fu.
