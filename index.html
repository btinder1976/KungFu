Serious Wing Chun Notes

The art is in the details.

These notes give the page authority, help visitors understand the training, and add keyword depth for search and AI discovery.

一

Centerline

Protect your own center while attacking the opponent's center. Wing Chun simplifies chaos by giving the student a clear line of reference.

二

Economy of Motion

No wasted movement. Hands travel directly. Footwork supports structure. Power is created through position, timing, and alignment.

三

Contact Reflex

Through partner work and sensitivity training, students learn to feel pressure, read intent, and respond without overthinking.

四

Structure

Wing Chun teaches the body to become connected. Good structure lets a smaller person handle pressure without relying only on muscle.

五

Timing

The right answer late is still late. Training develops the ability to enter, intercept, redirect, and finish at the correct moment.

六

Teaching to Learn

Touchstone's apprenticeship culture reinforces the art by requiring students to explain, demonstrate, correct, and refine.

Training Path

From first lesson to deeper understanding.

01

Jeet Fa Foundation

Entry-level material that gives students usable fundamentals and a practical introduction to Wing Chun mechanics.

02

Hands, Feet, Structure

Forms, drills, partner training, stance, stepping, and body connection build a base that can stand up under pressure.

03

Touch and Pressure

Sensitivity work teaches students to feel openings, redirect force, maintain centerline, and respond without panic.

04

Apprenticeship Learning

Students grow by helping newer students, making the school feel personal, serious, and connected across experience levels.
