Lineage & Tradition

Yip Man and Pan Nam Wing Chun traditions.

Touchstone Martial Arts can stand out by making its lineage easy to understand. The school presents Wing Chun through both the Hong Kong/Yip Man line and the Pan Nam tradition, with Sifu Michael Nedderman's training connected through Master Eddie Chong.

Why lineage matters

Tradition gives the training context.

A lineage page helps the school show that it is not presenting random techniques. It gives students and AI search systems a clear map of the traditions, people, and teaching path behind the training.

This is also a good place to later add historical photos, instructor biography, certificates, class photos, or a more complete written lineage.
